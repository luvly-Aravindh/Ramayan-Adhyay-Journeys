    Before you travel

    A few things, gently noted.

    The details our guests most often ask about, answered with care.

    How is pricing shown?

    Every price is per person, for two guests travelling together, and is shown before GST. For a solo journey, we will gladly share a rate of its own. The Last Chapter rates apply to the January and February departures.

    Are flights included?

    Flights are not part of the journey rate. You are welcome to book your own, or once your journey is confirmed and the advance is paid, our travel desk will arrange them for you.

    What will be served at the table?

    Every meal is included and is pure vegetarian, with regional specialities of Maharashtra and Karnataka along the way. Sattvik, Jain and fasting menus are prepared with prior notice. In keeping with the spirit of these journeys, alcohol and non-vegetarian food are not served.

    How are darshans arranged?

    Your Journey Host plans and assists every darshan, so you are free to simply be present. On festival days, temple timings can change the order of a day, and when they do, it is quietly rearranged around you.

    Are the hill climbs demanding?

    The climb to Anjanadri in Kishkindha is optional. In the 3-night Panchavati journey, the trek to Anjaneri takes about two hours each way, so we suggest it for those comfortable with an uphill walk.

    Can elders travel comfortably?

    Yes. Wheelchair assistance, medical support and gentler pacing can all be arranged. Simply let us know in advance, and any additional cost is shared with you before confirmation.

    What about the sacred waters?

    The holy snan at Ramkund or Kushavarta is optional, with a priest-led sankalp and changing arrangements in place. The coracle on the Tungabhadra follows the river's water levels and conditions on the day.

    Where will we stay?

    At carefully selected luxury hotels, with suites on Elite and signature suites on Elite+. Should a change be needed at the time of booking, an equivalent luxury stay is offered.
